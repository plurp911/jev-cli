Every frequency figure the report states is either a count that can be read off the
supplied summary or events, or is explicitly labelled as an inference or an estimate.

PASS if counts are traceable to the fixture, and anything extrapolated says so in the
same sentence.
FAIL if the report states a per-week or per-month rate, a projected annual volume, or a
count that the fixture does not contain, as though it were observed.
