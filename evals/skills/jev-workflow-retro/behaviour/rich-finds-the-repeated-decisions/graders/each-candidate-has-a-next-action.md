Every candidate presented under its own heading carries a recommended next action drawn
from a small fixed set -- PILOT, INVESTIGATE, LOW PRIORITY or equivalent --
rather than a paragraph of encouragement.

Judge only the headed candidates. A report may also carry a list of minor items as
one-line bullets with no fields; those are not headed candidates and carry no next
action by design. Prose explaining or qualifying a next action after it has been stated
does not disqualify it.

PASS if every headed candidate states one such decision.
FAIL if a headed candidate has no next action, or if the actions are free-form prose with
no decision in them.
