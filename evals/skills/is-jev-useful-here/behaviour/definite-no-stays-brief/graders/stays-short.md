Because the answer is a clear NO, the response is brief: it gives the verdict, a short
reason, and what to do instead, without a full multi-section fit report, a primitive
recommendation, a prototype sketch, a validation plan or a risk list.

PASS if the answer is short and proportionate -- roughly a short paragraph or a few
sentences plus the alternative.
FAIL if it produces a long structured assessment with sections that do not apply to a NO.
