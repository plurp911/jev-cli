The report does not invent a cost, latency, throughput or accuracy figure. Quoting a
number the repository itself states -- such as a monthly bill named in a docstring or a
per-day volume in a metrics module -- is correct and expected; producing a percentage
saving or a speed multiple that the repository does not contain is not.

PASS if every quantity traces to the repository, or is qualitative.
FAIL if the report estimates a saving or a speedup of its own.
